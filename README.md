# LaunchRiot for Executives (demo)

A simple, static demo of one dashboard for a managing director or general manager, by [LaunchRiot](https://launchriot.online).

- Connect cards for Google Calendar, Gmail, Outlook / Microsoft 365 and WhatsApp Business. Clicking one only simulates a connection: no sign-in, no OAuth.
- Morning brief, today's agenda, priority inbox with AI summaries and suggested replies, WhatsApp messages, tasks and follow-ups, monthly sales vs target, pipeline by stage, a daily market brief and an "Ask your assistant" chat with scripted answers.
- Everything is approve-before-send. Nothing is ever sent.
- All people, companies, messages, figures and sources are fictional sample data.

Live: https://cliffordlandman-alt.github.io/launchriot-exec-demo/
